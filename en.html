Table Score — Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026. Publisher: sogerg.

In short

Table Score is a scorekeeper for board games and card games. The app works entirely offline: no account, no ads, no analytics, no data sent to the publisher or to any third party.

Data stored on your device

  • The players you create: name (or nickname), colour and optional symbol.
  • Games: chosen game, players, settings, scores of each round, start and end dates.
  • The language chosen in the settings.

This data stays in the app's local storage on your phone. The publisher has no access to it. It is erased if you delete players or games in the app, or if you uninstall the app.

Exports and sharing you trigger

When you ask for it, the app creates a file on your device and opens the system share sheet: scoreboard image (PNG), export of a game (CSV) or full backup (JSON). You choose where it goes (messaging, email, storage…); the app never sends anything by itself.

Restoring a backup only reads the file you select in the system document picker.

Permissions

  • Vibration (Android): the phone vibrates when the timer ends.
  • Internet access (Android): permission declared by default by the app's framework (Expo). The app makes no network request; links (rule sources, this page) open in your browser.
  • No camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos permission is requested. The screen stays on in table mode and during the timer, which requires no permission.

Purchases

The current version contains no in-app purchase. If a one-time “Pro” purchase is added, payment will be handled by the App Store or Google Play, and this page will be updated before release.

Children

The app collects no personal data, whatever the user's age.

Trademarks and unofficial sheets

Score sheets are unofficial. Game names mentioned belong to their publishers; the app is not affiliated with or endorsed by them. A publisher who wants a sheet removed can ask through the contact link below and it will be taken down.
